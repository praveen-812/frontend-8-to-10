marriage

Marriage is a legally

, socially, or religiously sanctioned union between two individuals. It establishes mutual rights, obligations, property inheritance, and social status, while officially recognizing their commitment to share their lives together



legal union

In modern societies, a civil marriage acts as a legally binding contract regulated by the government. It dictates parameters for property rights, taxation, medical decisions, and, if applicable, the terms of divorce.



Social and Religious Union:

Beyond legalities, it is often celebrated with a wedding ceremony to mark a couple's commitment in the eyes of their family, community, or faith. Historically and culturally, it has been a primary structure for procreation, child-rearing, and the organization of families.
