AI Overview Swiggy’s staff rules and regulations are governed by their corporate Code of Conduct Policy, outlining expectations for full-time staff, contractors, and third-party vendors. The framework ensures ethical conduct, data security, and compliance with labor codes


Corporate and Operational Regulations


  • Conflict of Interest: Employees are prohibited from making business decisions involving third parties that employ, are owned by, or offer financial interest to a relative. Potential conflicts must be disclosed immediately.

  • Information Security: All workers must safeguard user data, mitigate security risks, and report any breaches directly to the Infosec team.

  • Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption: Employees, directors, and officers must follow strict procedures to identify and reject questionable transactions or behaviors

  • Leave and Expenses: Standard corporate employees are entitled to 22 annual leaves (14 paid leaves and 8 sick leaves). Business-related travel and lodging expenses are governed by the swiggy Reimburesement policy
