India features a robust and globally dominant information technology sector, with its premier firms listed on both the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). The landscape is led by Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), which ranks as the country's largest IT exporter and a major pillar of the market. Following closely is Infosys, a pioneer in global consulting and digital services headquartered in Bengaluru. Other large-cap heavyweights include HCL Technologies, renowned for its infrastructure and engineering services, Wipro, which delivers comprehensive business process and technology solutions, and Tech Mahindra, a leader in telecommunications and digital transformation software. Together, these publicly traded corporations command a massive share of India's total market capitalization, driving institutional investment and representing the nation's primary engine for technology employment and global service delivery
