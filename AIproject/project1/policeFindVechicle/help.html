📋 Standard Vehicle Scanning Procedure (SOP)

1

1. Open Vehicle Scanner

Navigate to the Vehicle Scan page on your mobile browser or terminal.

2

2. Start Camera or Upload Image

Grant camera access to align the number plate or upload a photo from storage.

3

3. Capture Number Plate

Hold steady and tap "Capture Plate Image" when the plate is centered in the HUD.

4

4. Confirm Detected Number

Verify the OCR recognized registration characters in the input field.

5

5. Select Check Vehicle Status

Trigger sub-second database cross-referencing against active theft registers.

6

6. Review Result Banner

Immediately inspect whether the screen turns RED (Stolen), GREEN (Clean), or YELLOW (Verification Required).

7

7. Follow Official Police Protocols

Verify physical vehicle details (Chassis / Engine number) and coordinate with the Division Control Room before enforcement.

🚦 Understanding Result Classifications

🔴

YES — STOLEN VEHICLE REPORT FOUND

An active FIR or stolen vehicle complaint matches this registration number in the central database. The officer should click "Notify Supervisor" and open the complete crime dossier to compare vehicle make, color, and owner details.

🟢

NO — NO STOLEN VEHICLE REPORT FOUND

No active theft record was found in the demo repository. Notice: This clearance does not guarantee vehicle ownership or insurance validity. Continue standard physical document checks.

🟡

VERIFICATION REQUIRED

The optical image was blurry, skewed, occluded, or contained an invalid format. Capture a new image under better lighting or type the registration number manually into the confirmation box.

🇮🇳 Indian Vehicle Registration Plate Format Reference

VehicleGuard AI normalizes and supports common Indian registration structures:

Standard State Format: [SS][DD][LL][NNNN]

Example: TN 09 AB 1234

  • TN: State code (Tamil Nadu)
  • 09: RTO district code (e.g. Chennai West)
  • AB: Series alphabet combination
  • 1234: 4-digit unique vehicle number
Bharat (BH) Series: [YY]BH[NNNN][LL]

Example: 22 BH 1234 AB

  • 22: Year of registration
  • BH: Bharat code
  • 1234: 4-digit unique registration number
  • AB: Series identification

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Is this connected to the live CCTNS or Parivahan database?

No. This system is a frontend technological prototype and demonstration platform. All vehicle records, complaints, and police station entries are simulated.

What should an officer do when a STOLEN alert is triggered?

Immediately note the physical details of the driver and vehicle, inspect the chassis number stamped on the vehicle frame, notify the Division Control Room, and verify official CCTNS records before detaining.

How does the camera OCR work on low-end mobile devices?

The frontend uses standard HTML5 media streams and client-side bounding box preview, followed by normalized text ingestion for rapid, lightweight processing.

Can I switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode?

Yes! Tap the Moon 🌙 / Sun ☀️ icon in the navigation bar to toggle between the tactical Police IT Dark Mode and administrative Light Mode.
