Taxonomy of Intelligence

The 3 Major Types of AI

Understanding current capabilities vs future frontiers of artificial intelligence.

Current Reality (Active)

Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)

Specialized systems engineered to excel at single tasks (e.g. ChatGPT text generation, Tesla Autopilot vision, AlphaFold protein folding, Siri voice parsing).

  • Domain-specific precision
  • Cannot transfer knowledge to unrelated domains
Active Research Frontier

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

Hypothetical systems capable of understanding, learning, and applying cognitive intelligence across any human intellectual task with zero-shot adaptability.

  • Cross-domain cognitive generalization
  • Reasoning & continuous self-learning
Theoretical Horizon

Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)

Intellect that surpasses the collective cognitive capacity of all human minds combined across scientific creativity, wisdom, and problem-solving.

  • Exponential algorithmic self-improvement
  • AI Safety & Alignment requirement
